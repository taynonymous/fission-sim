Stylized model. Fissile concentrations, delayed-neutron fractions (×8) and decay times are exaggerated so a reaction is visible with about 1,300 nuclei in two dimensions. Neutrons per fission (ν), energy per fission and delayed fractions shown in the fuel facts are real values.

Doppler feedback is modeled: as the fission rate climbs, the fuel heats up and U-238 or Th-232 capture more neutrons, which pulls k back down. Fast cores with no moderator get much less of this help.

k-effective is measured live as neutrons born from fission divided by neutrons lost to absorption or leakage over the last 3 seconds.
